# JJJCB-Medwell
The idea behind the P3 project for Medwell Solutions is to create a functioning prototype that can verify participation, award points, manage campaign content, and provide measurable reporting to partners.
Essentially, P3 encourages people to participate in and complete approved events, activities, and learning materials, then allows those running these initiatives to verify, track, and report on engagement.

Closing the gap between treatment and prevention in healthcare is the driving force behind P3. Currently, non-profits that focus on prevention and awareness are frequently underfunded to scale their influence,
while physicians and hospitals are largely in for the business. Pre-health students, on the other hand, have difficulty with mentorship and shadowing programs that would enable them to obtain practical
experience while contributing to their communities. By developing a mobile-friendly platform that motivates students and community members to participate in authorized prevention, health literacy, nonprofit
support, and community impact activities, P3 seeks to address these issues.
